How the metrics are calculated

CTR

Clicks ÷ Impressions.

Customers

Clicks × Conversion rate.

CAC

Spend ÷ Customers. Lower is better.

LTV : CAC

Customer lifetime value ÷ CAC. Above 3 is commonly treated as healthy; below 1 loses money per customer.

Modelled value

Customers × LTV. An estimate of lifetime value, not booked revenue.

ROI

(Modelled value − Spend) ÷ Spend.

Opportunity score

40% LTV:CAC (capped at 5×) + 20% conversion (capped at 5%) + 15% CTR (capped at 8%) + 25% headroom (share of search volume / audience not yet captured).

Projection

Customers = current customers × (new spend ÷ current spend)^elasticity. Elasticity below 1 means diminishing returns; it is editable per channel.
